## Known issues

1. **Lab performance below budget** (guide §5: performance ≥ 95, LCP ≤ 2.0 s). LCP is 3.0–3.7 s in the simulated mobile lab. The LCP images load early (resource delay under 30 ms); most of the time is element render delay (0.4–1.3 s) behind main-thread work, mainly hydrating the React/Next runtime. Next step: Speed Insights field data after launch, then cut client components from the shell and defer non-critical ones. Not a blocker for launch.
2. Vercel deploys `main` to production without waiting for CI until Deployment Checks are on (`docs/ops/vercel-setup.md` section 9).
