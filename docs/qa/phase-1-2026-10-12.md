# Phase 1 gate (task 1.G): Folio template

Date: 2026-10-12. Auditor: qa agent. Build audited: `main` at `3799b92` (tasks 1.1–1.7 and 1.9 merged; 1.8 screenshots still pending, so missing covers, phone shots, avatar, BibTeX and the Scholar link are known content gaps and are not counted as defects).
Servers: Folio `SITE_TEMPLATE=folio NEXT_DIST_DIR=.next-folio next start -p 3601`, Editorial `next start -p 3600` (regression check only). Both were stopped after the audit.

## Verdict: PASS WITH ISSUES

There are **0 blockers, 2 major and 19 minor** findings.

- All gates are green: lint, typegen + tsc, 50 unit tests, both builds, and 225/225 e2e tests in 126 s.
- axe found **0 violations** of any impact in 25 scans (every page and state at 360 and 1440, reduced motion, menu open).
- There is no horizontal scroll at any width, no tap target under 44 px, no input under 16 px, and no lime text on a light background.
- The 1.4 and 1.7 behaviour was verified in a real browser: the hover modal, the preloader, the crossfade, the menu after a transition, scroll restoration, the 404 page, the compact footer and `aria-current`.
- Lighthouse a11y, best practices and SEO are 100 on every page.

Two things keep this from a clean PASS:

- **Home scores Lighthouse mobile 93** (5 runs: 93/93/96/93/93), below the 95 that task 1.4 requires.
- **LCP is over 2.0 s on every page** (2.56–3.01 s). That is better than Editorial's 3.24 s baseline, but still outside guide §5.11.

Under the work plan, the phase can close with no blockers. The two major findings should be fixed or explicitly deferred to 5.2 by the lead.

**What limits LCP now:** the LCP elements themselves. Every Folio page beats the Editorial baseline (LCP −0.2 to −0.7 s; a11y 96 → 100) and all are free of layout shift. The remaining 0.6–1.0 s over budget is transfer contention on the simulated 1.6 Mbps link:
- the 98 KB variable font preload;
- ~157 KB of script (framework 130.5 KB of it);
- render-blocking CSS (~18 KB gzip in 3 files).

The LCP element's own load and render are ≤ 0.25 s. The levers are those of work plan 5.2: subset or static-cut the font (L13), drop Editorial from the registry (−10.5 KB), and inline or split the critical CSS. On Home the preloader also costs about 2 s of Speed Index on a first visit (F-01).

Other Lighthouse items on every Folio page: `legacy-javascript` est. 14 KiB (the same polyfills as the baseline) and `unused-javascript` est. 26–27 KiB. Editorial's mobile Lighthouse run also downloads GSAP and Lenis, because Lighthouse's mobile emulation reports `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`. Folio's motion loader is idle-deferred and was not fetched within the Lighthouse trace.
